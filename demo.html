How it works

MediaPipe detects the hand, crops its bounding box and resizes it to a 28 × 28 grayscale image. TensorFlow.js runs the original CNN weights and predicts one of 24 static ASL letters. Predictions below 80% confidence are rejected. This is a fingerspelling prototype, not a complete sign language translation system; dynamic letters J and Z are not supported. Lighting, camera angle and background can affect results.
